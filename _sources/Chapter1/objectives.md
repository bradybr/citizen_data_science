# Objectives

To close the loop on this Introductory section, let's revisit the stated theme of this book.  We want to expose the reader to the proper way to go about finding value through analytics.  This is not by focusing on the fancy buzz words and complicated methodologies.  If we start first by understanding the types of problems we solve and build up our analytical muscles around being able to identify good problems to solve, then we've actually done most of the heavy lifting believe it or not.  

After we know what a worthwhile project looks like, we want to focus on exposing the reader to high-level mathematical concepts, but with just enough detail to enable practical usage for the motivated business associate.  There's a minimum level of understanding we must attain to ensure we keep you safe from failing prey to common mistakes and misinterpreting results, but it is possible to reach this level with some effort and practice.

Overall, we hope to take some of the specialized knowledge from the advanced analytics practioners toolbox, pare down what they know in their expertise and methodologies to just the essentials, in an easy to understand manner, while simulatenosuly elevating the front-line business analyst to be able to operate at this minimum level.  This is our goal.

As a practicum and secondary level education course, we must be intentional in the material we aim to cover however.  Students will likely not have fulfilled many of the minimum mathematics requirements in algebra, probability, and statistics prior to this course to enable a super robust deep dive into certain subjects.  Lastly, due to the restrictions of time, there's only so much we could reasonably expect to cover during a semester.

Taking all of this into account, the following are our objectives for this course.

1. Create a foundational understanding of data science and machine learning concepts
2. Advance from novice to intermediate skills in the KNIME analytics platform
3. Develop an experiential understanding of the business applications and value potential working with these concepts and skills
