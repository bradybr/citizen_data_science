It's now time to introduce the hero of our story.

Recall the subtitle of this course, _Anyone Can Find Value Through Advanced Analytics_.  It does not take an astronomical investment or building a team of PhDs from MIT to build a robust analytical community within an organization.  Chances are you already have the talent working in-role right now.  It simply takes an investment in time and energy to unlock and enhance what these resources are capable of.

The notion of a citizen data scientist is easy for me to wrap my head around because I lived it.  The first 10 years of my career was on the business side of our operations.  I worked in business analyst roles embedded within a number of different business functions, Order to Cash, Supply Chain Logistics, Sales, and Customer Analytics.  In all of these roles, my primary responsibilities were to first support our operations through analytics.  Honestly, it really made no difference what function it was, the process was the same.
