# <span style="color:white">Beginner's Guide To Citizen Data Science</span>

Welcome to the course reading material for BUS152 - Business Practicum II.

Introduction to Citizen Data Science and Machine Learning methodologies, theory, mathematics, and real-world applications in making predictions, forecasting, statistical analysis, and optimization. Emphasis on hands-on labs/exercises and the practical development and execution of analytical business use cases for business analysts and associates.

<br>

Coupled with the KNIME exercises and labs, and the project documentation found in the public github repository, this ebook makes up the entirety of the supplemental material for this course.
